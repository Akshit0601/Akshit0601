## 👋 About Me

Hi, I'm **Akshit**, currently pursuing my master's in Electrical and Computer Engineering at **UC San Diego**, specializing in Intelligent Systems, Robotics and Control. I love robotics and perception systems, and I enjoy building algorithms that draw their intuition from the human way of thinking and learning, and how they can be deployed for a real-life problem.
